Brain for repair and verify

Tell it (the agent that does a task from your words)
Every page the agent reads stays on this computer: intranet, CRM, HR portal, bank, e-mail. Needs an agent model served by llama-server, e.g. Qwen3.5-4B (8 GB Macs) or Qwen3.5-9B (16 GB or more, stronger): llama-server -m Qwen3.5-4B-Instruct-Q4_K_M.gguf --port 8080. The small 2B model AWOS installs for repair / verify cannot drive a page by itself.
