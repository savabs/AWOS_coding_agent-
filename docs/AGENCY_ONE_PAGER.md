## What we deploy

**AWOS** — a learning agent that runs on your repos.

- Routes to cheap models first, escalates only on failure (you never pick models)
- Tracks every mistake in a local `.awos/` database — compiles fixes into policy
- Delivers a **PEI scorecard** weekly: quality, speed, cost per project
- Gets faster and cheaper every client project

Not an IDE. Not Cursor. Infrastructure that learns your agency.
