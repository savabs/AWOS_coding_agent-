# Mac-only product: market size and willingness to pay (research, 2026-10-22)

Research agent, web sources. Caveats are at the end.

## Bottom line

Mac-only is a **niche, not a mass market**:
- about **6M** Mac-first small businesses and professionals across the US, EU and UK;
- of those, roughly **60k–160k** would plausibly pay $29–99 a month for admin automation;
- a realistic start is **1k–15k paying customers**, about **$0.6M–$10M a year** in recurring revenue.

Two limits:
1. **Mac share** is about 25% in the US and UK but only about 16% in the EU.
2. **No Mac-only prosumer tool charges $29–99 a month.** Mac utilities sit at $3–10 a month. That price only works if the product is priced as bookkeeping or admin software (Dext is about $25 a month) on hours saved.

## Mac share

StatCounter, September 2026 web traffic:

| Region | Mac share |
|---|---|
| US desktop | about 26.5% (Windows 64.5%) |
| UK | about 24.7% |
| Europe | about 16.5% |
| Global | about 16.8% |

Other surveys:
- **SMEs (JumpCloud IT admins):** 22% → 24% → 27% from 2024 to January 2025, rising. Skews toward tech companies.
- **US enterprise:** Omdia FY2025 puts Apple at 11% (+2.4 points). IDC: about 5% of global commercial shipments; 16% of the US PC market.
- **Lawyers:** ABA 2015, 8.1% overall and 15.5% of solos. Clio's 55–66% comes from biased samples.
- **Realtors:** NAR 2013–14, 14% Mac.
- **Developers:** about 33% professional Mac use (Stack Overflow 2022).
- **No data found** for freelancers, creatives, therapists, agencies or consultants.

## Willingness to pay

No rigorous study compares Mac and Windows software spending.
- **Setapp survey:** Mac users report an app budget of about $139 a year.
- **macapps.report:** third-party Mac software is worth roughly $3–6B a year, or about $20–50 per Mac (low confidence).

Comparable Mac software businesses:

| Product | Users / revenue | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Raycast | Hundreds of thousands of daily users; raised $30M Series B in 2024 (~$48M total) | Pro $10/mo, Plus $20, Max $50; Teams $15–65 per user |
| Setapp (MacPaw) | 12.5k paying users and ~$1.5M ARR (2017); 1M users (2019) | $9.99/mo |
| MacPaw | >$23M (2018); $35–90M (2024–25 estimates, conflicting) | — |
| Readdle / Spark | 19M+ users; est. $25–50M revenue | Freemium |
| TextExpander | 100k+ monthly active users; raised $41.4M (2022) | ~$3–4/mo |
| Things, Keyboard Maestro, CleanShot X | Mostly indie businesses under $5M a year | Mostly one-time purchase |
| Dext (cross-platform category comparable) | — | ~$25/mo; already fetches supplier-portal invoices |

Only MacPaw, Readdle and probably Raycast are clearly in the tens of millions.

**Owners who do their own books:** 46% of US owners have no accountant; in Canada only 38% use an accountant or bookkeeper. Hubdoc dropped bank auto-fetch in 2022; Dext Fetch still pulls supplier invoices weekly.

## Sizing assumptions

1. **Small businesses:** about 68M across the US (36.2M), UK (5.7M) and EU (26.1M).
2. **With real, recurring admin across several portals:** about 30M.
3. **Mac as the main machine** (25% US/UK, 15% EU): about **6.3M**.
4. **Five or more portals to log into each month** (50%): about 3M.
5. **Would pay $29–99/mo** (2–5%, the weakest assumption): **60k–160k**.
6. **Realistic early capture** (1–10% at ~$50/mo): **$0.6M–$10M ARR**.

## Caveats and warning signs

- **StatCounter** measures web traffic, not who owns a business.
- **JumpCloud** is a vendor survey.
- **Data by profession** is thin and old.
- **Private-company revenue** figures are aggregator guesses.
- **Bookkeepers and accountants mostly use Windows**, so word of mouth through them is limited.
- **Competition:** MacPaw already ships **Eney**, a local-first Mac assistant ("computerbeing"), in beta since 2025 on Apple Silicon.
- **Shutdowns:** MacPaw closed Setapp Mobile (Feb 2026) and CleanMyMac Business (Jul 2026), a hint that Mac business products are hard.
- **Best US/UK fit:** a solo or micro professional who does their own admin on a Mac.
