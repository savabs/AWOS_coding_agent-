# Where to post first, and who to DM

Rules for all of these:
- The owner posts and DMs personally, one at a time, and writes each post for that place.
- Read the community's rules and the pinned self-promo policy **before** posting. When in doubt, ask a mod first.
- No cold mass DMs. DM only people who have publicly complained about a specific chore, or who you already know. One message each, and at most one follow-up.
- Don't cross-post the same text everywhere on the same day. Space it out over the week (README.md has the order).
- Answer every reply. Disclose that you're the builder every time.
- Use the right `?src=` tag (README.md).
