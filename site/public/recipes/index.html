Recipes

Chores other people handed off

A recipe is a recorded chore with the personal data taken out. Open one to read every step in plain language, then add it to AWOS Replay and run it on your own account.

These six are examples on made-up sites (example.com and friends), so you can see what a recipe looks like. Record your own on the sites you use, then share it with Share as link in AWOS Replay.

Bills · billing.example.com · 4 stepsDownload this month's invoiceOpens the billing page of a utility account, picks the month you name and saves the invoice PDF to Downloads. Reports · dashboard.example.com · 5 stepsExport last week's sales as CSVOpens the reports area of a shop dashboard, sets the date range to last week and exports the sales table as a CSV file. Forms · hr.example.org · 9 stepsFill the weekly timesheet, stop before SubmitFills a standard 5 x 8 hour week on a timesheet portal and stops on the review screen, so you check it and press Submit yourself. Orders · shop.example.net · 4 stepsCheck an order's delivery statusLooks up an order number on a store's order-tracking page and reads back the current status. Alerts · jobs.example.com · 5 stepsNew job listings for a searchRuns a job search sorted by newest, limited to the last 7 days, so you see what was posted since last time. Inventory · admin.example.org · 5 stepsUpdate stock for one productFinds a product by its SKU in a store back office and sets the stock count. It stops for your OK before saving.
