Pricing

AWOS Pro

AWOS runs on your machine with your own Anthropic key or a local model. You always pay your model provider directly. Pro removes the monthly limit on cloud-model jobs.

Free
  • Local-model jobs: unlimited
  • Cloud-model jobs: 25 a month, counted on your machine
  • Everything else in AWOS
Pro
  • Cloud-model jobs: unlimited
  • Local-model jobs: unlimited
  • Cancel any time; access runs to the end of the period you paid for

After you pay

The next page shows your licence key. Activate it once on each machine:

awos activate awos_XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX
awos licence status

AWOS checks the key with this site at most once a day and keeps working offline for 14 days. Payments are handled by Paddle, our reseller and merchant of record. Usage counts are a separate, opt-in setting: see what AWOS sends.
