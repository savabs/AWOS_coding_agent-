Recipes

Chores other people handed off

A recipe is a recorded chore with the personal data taken out. Open one to read every step in plain language, then add it to AWOS Replay and run it on your own account.

These six are examples on made-up sites (example.com and friends), so you can see what a recipe looks like. Record your own on the sites you use, then share it with Share as link in AWOS Replay.

Make your own

Install AWOS Replay, press record, do the chore once. Anything you mark "ask me" or that looks like a password is left out of the recipe.
