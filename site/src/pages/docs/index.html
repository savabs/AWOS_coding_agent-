AWOS documentation

AWOS is a background coding agent that runs on your own machine. You queue small, well-defined tickets; a local worker takes them one at a time, works in a separate copy of your repo, checks the result, and leaves a local review branch with a short report.

The workflow in one picture

  1. Queue. awos submit --repo . "GOAL" adds a job with a dollar cap to a local queue.
  2. Run. awos serve --worker takes queued jobs one at a time.
  3. Isolate. Each job works in its own git worktree at your current commit.
  4. Verify. The worker runs your tests in that workspace and checks the result against the goal.
  5. Hand off. The diff becomes a local branch awos/<id> with report.md. Nothing is pushed.
  6. Review. awos status --digest shows what finished, what is blocked, and what was spent today.
