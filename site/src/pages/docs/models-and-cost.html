Models and cost

You bring your own model provider and pay them directly. AWOS records the cost of every model call and stops a job at its budget.

Cloud: Claude Haiku 5.5

The default cloud model is Claude Haiku 5.5, called directly through the Anthropic API with prompt caching on. Set ANTHROPIC_API_KEY and check it with awos keys.

Local: a free model on your machine

Run an open model on your own computer and point AWOS at it with AWOS_PROVIDER=local. AWOS talks to a local OpenAI-compatible server such as llama-server; awos keys reports whether it is reachable. A local job costs $0 per ticket and needs no network.

  • Measured: Qwen3.5 9B on a 16 GB MacBook solved 8 of 10 easy real issues through AWOS (2026-10).
  • It is slow: about 20 minutes or more per issue on that laptop, so it suits overnight queues of routine fixes.
  • Combine it with --privacy local_only to guarantee a job cannot reach a cloud model (see Security).

Budgets

  • awos submit --budget USD caps a job's spend. The default is 1.0; 0 means no cap.
  • The cap covers the whole job, including any continuation: each attempt gets what is left.
  • A job stops when it reaches its cap.
  • awos status --digest shows today's spend, counted from AWOS's own model calls.
