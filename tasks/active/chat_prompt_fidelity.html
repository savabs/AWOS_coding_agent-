Decisions

  • Do not expose hidden chain-of-thought; expose observable prompt/context metadata and assumptions only.
  • Keep the renderer collapsed by default to preserve a simple programmer-oriented UI.
  • Provider adapters remain replaceable; the contract is provider-neutral.
